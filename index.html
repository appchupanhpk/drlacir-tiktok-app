Short-video production and publishing for one TikTok account

Drlacir TikTok Auto Posting is an internal tool operated by Pham Kim Group (Vietnam) to produce short vertical videos about marriage and relationships for its own TikTok account @drlacir.hn.nhn.kh (“Drlacir Hôn nhân không ai dạy!”), and to send those videos to that account through TikTok’s official Content Posting API after a person has reviewed them.

What the tool does

1. Writes the content

Drafts a headline, short text, caption and hashtags in Vietnamese, checks them against the channel’s style rules and against previously produced posts to avoid repetition.

2. Creates the visuals

Generates background images with open-source AI image models and adds camera-style motion. No third-party photos or videos are used as input.

3. Adds music

Uses instrumental music composed by the tool itself, chosen to match the mood of the text.

4. Renders the video

Produces a 1080 × 1920 MP4 with the text overlaid, then places it in a review queue.

How TikTok is used

  1. The owner of @drlacir.hn.nhn.kh signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorises the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.
  2. Before uploading, the tool reads the creator information returned by the Content Posting API to confirm that it is connected to @drlacir.hn.nhn.kh and nothing else.
  3. A video is uploaded only after a team member has approved it in the review queue. By default it is sent as a private post or to the TikTok inbox as a draft, so the account owner makes the final decision in the TikTok app.
  4. Videos with AI-generated visuals are marked as AI-generated content when posted.

This tool is not offered to other TikTok users. It connects to one account owned by its operator and does not read, collect or analyse content or data from any other TikTok user.
